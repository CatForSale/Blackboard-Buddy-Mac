Blackboard Buddy for Mac
========================

1. Unzip, then drag "Blackboard Buddy" into your Applications folder.

2. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app, because it isn't from the App Store.
   Open it once anyway:
   - Double-click it and close the message.
   - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Blackboard Buddy.
   After that it opens normally.

3. Signing in to Blackboard uses Google Chrome (or Microsoft Edge). If neither is on your Mac,
   install Chrome from google.com/chrome first.

Your settings, notes and study files are kept in:
   ~/Library/Application Support/BlackboardBuddy

New version? The app tells you and opens the download page. Unzip it and drag the new app into
Applications, replacing the old one. Your notes and settings stay.

To remove it: quit the app, drag it from Applications to the Bin, and delete the folder above if you
don't want your data either.
